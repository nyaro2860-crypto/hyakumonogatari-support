An iPhone app that maps Japanese legends, yokai, folktales and ghost stories as little diorama characters. Light a lantern at each place you visit and collect a hundred tales. Your records stay on your iPhone.

About the legends

These are legends and rumors, not claims that the supernatural is real. Each place shows its source (local government or tourism pages, Wikipedia, etc.). Text quoted from Wikipedia is shown under CC BY-SA 4.0. If you find a mistake, please let us know.

When visiting

Many places are shrines, temples or people's neighborhoods. Please visit quietly in daytime and never enter restricted areas. Places marked "approximate" are not exact locations.

Routes don't appear

Routes use Apple's directions service and need an internet connection. The map and stories still work offline.

Restoring purchases

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Visited → Lantern Log → Shop → Restore purchases.
