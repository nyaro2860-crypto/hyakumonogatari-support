Privacy Policy

Effective: November 11, 2026

1. Information we collect

The developer of Hyakumonogatari Map ("the app") does not collect personal information. There are no accounts and no developer-operated servers.

2. Where your records are stored

Visited places, dates, journal photos, wish list and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer or third parties. If iCloud Backup is on, they may be included in your device backup (encrypted by Apple).

3. Location

If you allow it, your location is used on your device to show where you are, show distances to legends, and light "on-site" lanterns. It is never sent to the developer.

Only when you use Route or Nearby Legend Tour, your current location and the destination coordinates are sent to Apple's directions service to calculate the route. Maps are also provided by Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to these services.

4. Photos

Only photos you choose for the journal are read and saved on your device. They are never uploaded.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your name or payment details. Purchase status is checked on your device.

6. Ads, analytics and tracking

The app uses no ads, no analytics tools and no tracking.

7. External pages

Source links open external websites, which follow their own policies.

8. Deleting data

You can remove visits and photos in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all its data from your device.

9. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
